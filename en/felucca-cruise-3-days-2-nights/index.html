3-day felucca cruise from Aswan to Kom Ombo

Three days and two nights on the Nile: the islands of Aswan, the villages, the Daraw market if the day is right and the temple of Kom Ombo. The best choice for a first felucca trip, or if you only have a few days in Aswan.

Price

The whole boat for your group, all meals included. The rate depends on the season and the number of people: Tarek sends it on WhatsApp within minutes.

Ask Tarek for the price

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Aswan, the islands, first night on the river

    Tarek meets you around 9 am at your hotel or at the landing. Then the boat sails past Elephantine, Kitchener's Island, the Aga Khan mausoleum and the Tombs of the Nobles. Lunch on board. In the afternoon the city gives way to villages and palm trees. Mooring at an island before sunset, dinner, night on deck.

  2. Day 2

    Daraw and Kom Ombo

    Tea at sunrise, breakfast, sailing. If it is a market day, a short walk ashore to the camel market at Daraw. Then on towards Kom Ombo and its double temple, visited late in the afternoon when the light is soft and the groups have gone. Night moored near Kom Ombo.

  3. Day 3

    Last morning on the water

    A last stretch of sailing, breakfast on board, and you land around midday. From there, a car back to Aswan (about one hour), or on to Luxor with a stop at the temple of Edfu (about three and a half hours with the visit).

The wind decides the last stop. If it does not get us there, the last stretch is done by road.

Before you go

  • Meeting around 9 am at your hotel or at the landing in Aswan.
  • Bring a small day bag, a warm layer, a head torch, sunscreen, a power bank and cash in Egyptian pounds.

The route, from Aswan downstream

The Nile flows north, so from Aswan you sail downstream. Here are the places on the way. How far we get depends on the wind: if it does not get us to the last stop, the last stretch is done by road.

  1. Aswan and Elephantine

    Departure among the granite islands of the first cataract. You pass Elephantine, the botanical garden on Kitchener's Island, the Aga Khan mausoleum and the Tombs of the Nobles cut into the hill of Qubbet el-Hawa.

  2. Villages and islands

    North of Aswan the city disappears quickly. Small villages, palm trees on the banks, and the first night moored at an island or a sandy beach.

  3. Daraw

    A market town known for its camel market, held on certain days of the week. Tell Tarek your dates: he will tell you whether the trip can fall on a market day.

  4. Kom Ombo

    A double temple built for Sobek, the crocodile god, and Haroeris, standing right on the riverbank. Next to it, a small museum of mummified crocodiles. Best visited late in the afternoon.
